The structure of this International Standard is described in ISO 10303-1.

The numbering of the parts of the International Standard reflects its structure:
- Part 1 Overview and fundamental principles,
- Part 2 Vocabulary,
- Parts 11 to 19 specify the description methods,
- Parts 21 to 29 specify the implementation methods,
- Parts 31 to 39 specify the conformance testing methodology and framework,
- Parts 41 to 99 specify the integrated generic resources, provided in the SMRL,
- Parts 101 to 199 specify the integrated application resources, provided in the SMRL,
- Parts 201 to 299 specify the application protocols,
- Parts 301 to 399 specify the abstract test suites,
- Part 400 specifies the Reference schema for sysml mapping, provided in the SMRL,
- Parts 401 to 499 specify the application protocol modules, provided in the SMRL,
- Parts 501 to 599 specify the application interpreted constructs, provided in the SMRL,
- Parts 1001 to 1999 specify the application modules, provided in the SMRL,
- Parts 3001 to 3099 specify business object models, provided in the SMRL,
- Part 4000 specifies the Core model, provided in the SMRL,
- Parts 4401 to 4499 specify domain models, provided in the SMRL, and
- Parts 5001 to 5999 specify usage guides.
