www.file-extension.info/format/catpart     2026-08-21T00:00:00.0000000
A CATPART file is a 3D CAD part document from CATIA V5 (or V6/3DEXPERIENCE) by Dassault Systèmes - it stores one component's geometry and parametric feature history. To open it without a CATIA license, use the free eDrawings viewer or CAD Assistant. To use the part in other CAD software, export it to STEP.
