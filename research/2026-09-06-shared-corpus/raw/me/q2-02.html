A closer look at Pronom signatures - Open Preservation Foundation

openpreservation.org/blogs/closer-look-pronom-signatures/
're' is a simple regular expression that will match all of the PUIDS in the signature file. They look like PUID="fmt/11" or PUID="x-fmt/91". The parentheses save a portion of the match, so that we can access them using the special Bash shell variable BASH_REMATCH. The loop reads each line in turn.
