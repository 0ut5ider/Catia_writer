3D XML

Quickly and easily capture and share live and accurate 3D data in the universal lightweight 3DXML format.

3D XML Products
CATIA 3DEXPERIENCE

Using Dassault’s XCAD technology platform we are able to provide a broad range of direct CAD translation solutions which will work On Premise or in the Cloud.

3DEXPERIENCE Products
CATIA V5

Our wide range of CATIA solutions for direct CAD translation are built on top of Dassault’s XCAD technology ensuring the highest fidelity solutions.

CATIA V5 Products
CREO

Partnering with PTC and using the Creo API’s, ensures that our direct CAD translation solutions for Creo deliver the highest quality results supported by PTC.

CREO Products
Creo View

Our Creo View adapters can be integrated into Windchill, DIVISION Graphics Server or other PDM environments.

Creo View Adapters
ICEM

ICEM being a specialist solution means only a vendor supported solution can deliver the direct translation of complex surface data to other major CAD systems.

ICEM Surf Products >
INVENTOR

Inventor as a widely used system in the supply chain and at OEMS, needs a direct translation solution and using JT as a data exchange format delivers that in a self-contained product.

Inventor Products
JT

Create JT data from native CAD but also take JT data and create native CAD data and STEP files

JT Products
NX

Using Siemens NX Open tools enables us to deliver a broad set of high fidelity CAD to CAD translation products supported by the vendor.

NX Products
SOLIDWORKS

Being one of the most widely used CAD systems in the supply chain demands a broad use, direct translation solution and using JT as a data exchange format delivers that.

SOLIDWORKS Products
STEP

For companies wanting a neutral data translation solution, STEP AP 203/214 and AP 242 provides a mature ISO/ANSI mechanism for sharing MCAD data.

STEP Products
LEGACY PRODUCTS

Many long standing users of CAD still have legacy CAD data in the CADDS and CATIA V4 formats and we provide products and solutions to deliver migration solutions to address that requirement.

CADDS and CATIA V4 Products
